# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — 2026-09-19

- Added the game's standard live gold counter and animated `+amount` feedback
  while the player inventory is open.
- Added the enabled-by-default `show_gold_feedback` setting.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-19

- Initial release.
- Replaces the player-inventory trash button with immediate selling at the
  item's normal shipping-bin value.
- Supports selling one held item or a full held stack with Shift.
- Protects zero-value items from accidental deletion.
